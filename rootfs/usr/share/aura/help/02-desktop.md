# The AURA Desktop

## Windows

- Drag a window to the **left/right edge** to snap it to half the screen,
  or to the **top** to maximize.
- Window buttons (close · minimize · maximize) sit at the **top-left**.
- **Super + ←/→** tiles a window, **Super + F** fullscreens, **Super + W**
  closes, **Super + M** minimizes.
- Double-click a title bar to maximize/restore.

## Workspaces

AURA gives you four virtual desktops — Summit, Ridge, Valley and Glacier.
Switch with **Ctrl + Alt + ←/→**; drag window title bars to another edge
to carry windows along. Rename them in Settings → Workspaces.

## The dock

- Right-click the dock → Dock Preferences for size, position and zoom —
  or use `aura dock size 64` in Celestia Shell.
- Drag an app from the AURA menu onto the dock to pin it.
- The Trash sits at the end of the dock; drag files onto it.

## Notifications & clipboard

Notifications appear top-right and fade gently. Press **Super + V** for
clipboard history — everything you've copied recently, ready to paste.

## Screenshots

- **Print Screen** — whole screen (saved to ~/Pictures)
- **Shift + Print** — select a region
- **Alt + Print** — current window
- Or open **AURA Screenshot** for timers and more options.
